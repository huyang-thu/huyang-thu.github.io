Yang Hu 胡杨

Associate Professor · Doctoral Supervisor

School of Integrated Circuits
Tsinghua University

I work on computer architecture and AI chips, large-model training and inference systems, and mapping and design automation. My research connects parallel execution, compilers, and hardware to address the computation, memory, and communication demands of AI, with wafer-scale computing as a central theme.

I joined Tsinghua in 2022. Previously, I was a tenure-track Assistant Professor at The University of Texas at Dallas (2017–2021). I received my Ph.D. from the University of Florida in 2017, my M.S. from Tsinghua University in 2011, and my B.S. from Tianjin University in 2007.

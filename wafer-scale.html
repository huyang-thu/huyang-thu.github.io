Research theme

Wafer-Scale AI Computing

How can AI training and inference scale when compute, memory and communication demands grow together?

The research questionThree independent studiesEvaluation

Using communication to rethink scaling.

Wafer-scale integration opens a large space of compute, memory, and network choices. Useful performance depends on how workloads use those resources. The following studies address different decisions within this space.
